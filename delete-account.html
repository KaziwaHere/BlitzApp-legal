Blitz-sport Account Deletion

Blitz-sport allows users to permanently delete their accounts directly through the mobile application.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Blitz-sport application.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Navigate to your Profile or Account Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your request.

Data deletion

When you confirm account deletion, your account and associated personal information are deleted from our systems, subject to any data retention obligations required by law.
